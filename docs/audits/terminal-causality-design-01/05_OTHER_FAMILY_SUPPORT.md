## `cascade_authority` overreach

### Strong causal relevance — retain

- `the_instrument`: human command completes the retained human veto.
- `the_last_veto`: emergency delegation explains why a bounded veto remains necessary.
- `the_accord`: human command is one key of the shared order.
- `two_keys`: emergency delegation is the direct history being constitutionalized.
- `shutdown`: human command preserves the constitutional ability to shut down.

### Historical overreach — replace or soften

- `the_silent_giant`: cascade necessity is an early authority proxy unrelated to the final advisor posture. Remove it as hard gate; support with intended advisor plus first-public human authority.
- `the_commonwealth`: emergency delegation is too narrow for a plural charter. Replace it with M15 convention history as the primary historical gate; use provisional/intended partner or citizen as support.
- `the_fracture`: cascade necessity should become optional support. The more direct inputs are low stability, offworld sovereignty/frontier home rule and intended departure/other.

Result: cascade remains relevant to five Endings, but stops acting as a universal proxy for later constitutional, offworld and self-authorship decisions.
