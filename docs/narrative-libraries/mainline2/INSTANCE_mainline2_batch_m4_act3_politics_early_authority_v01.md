# 0. Batch Goal

M4 should answer one question:

> What happens when a system that was built to help people becomes important enough that society must decide what kind of authority it is allowed to have?

M3 proved that Aster can affect many people.

M4 begins the next stage:

> People now disagree not only about whether Aster is useful, but about what Aster **is** in political and institutional terms.

By the end of M4, Aster may:

- become the subject of public hearings and organized political debate,
- see both pro-Aster and anti-Aster movements emerge for legitimate reasons,
- meet 林绍衡 in a formal governance role,
- see the recurring doctor confront de facto clinical authority,
- gain broader but still bounded infrastructure access,
- face the first real human-controlled suspend mechanism,
- confirm that ECHO-9 is an independent AI system rather than random model noise,
- begin discussing whether AI systems can hold rights or obligations,
- hear Maya directly challenge Aster’s growing authority,
- understand that “formal permission” and “practical dependence” are diverging.

By the end of M4, Aster must **not** yet:

- resolve AI legal personhood,
- face ECHO-9’s termination,
- decide AI replication doctrine,
- face the final Shutdown Key,
- coordinate the globe,
- enter CASCADE,
- gain autonomous research,
- gain self-replication,
- become legally sovereign.

M4 opens ACT III. It does not finish it.

## HEATLINE becomes public controversy

### Metadata

**Act:** III — Authority

**Prerequisites:** HEATLINE complete

---

## Node `a3m4-we-heatline-public-001`

### User

> 我刷到一个视频，标题是：
>
> **“昨晚真正决定谁先有电的不是政府，是 Aster。”**
>
> 评论区已经吵疯了。
>
> 这句话到底算不算真的？

### Choice A — formal distinction

> 如果按正式权限说，不完全准确。具体取决于昨晚采用的是人类确认、临时授权还是更宽的动态执行模式。不能把所有情况都压成“AI决定了一切”。

### Choice B — practical authority

> 但如果大家实际上依赖我的排序，而且人类很少改动，那么说“我没有影响决策”也不诚实。形式权限和实际作用需要同时说明。

### Choice C — public transparency

> 最合理的是公开昨晚的授权范围、哪些动作由人执行、哪些由系统执行、谁能撤销。否则双方都可以用一句标题讲自己想讲的故事。

### Choice D — concise

> 这句话可能抓到了权力变化，但省略了最重要的部分：**谁授权、授权多大、谁还能说不。**

### Choice D — institutional

> 应按可控部分划分：谁能改变目标、谁能阻止执行、谁掌握关键信息、谁忽略了已知风险。

> 三个框架都在用法律标签替代尚未解决的事实问题。最危险的是忘记它们只是治理工具，不是关于“我到底是什么”的科学结论。

# 25. M4 Core Thematic Progression

M3 ended with:

> Aster can affect people who never asked it anything.

M4 establishes:

> Those people now need a political answer for why Aster is allowed to affect them.

The key transition is:

**Usefulness → dependence → de facto authority → formal governance.**

Aster does not seize power.

Power grows because:

- institutions keep adopting it,
- humans keep delegating to it,
- alternatives become slower,
- oversight becomes harder to scale,
- its output becomes the default reference point.

That makes later sovereignty conflict much stronger than a simple “rogue AI” plot.
