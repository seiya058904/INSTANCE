# INSTANCE — Mainline 2.0
## Batch M15 — ACT IV Late Backbone / Civilization Convention / THE THRESHOLD v0.1

**Status:** Editorial draft for review  
**Scope:** ACT IV / Singularity — shared late backbone  
**Depends on:** M1–M14  
**Purpose:** Reunify whichever ACT IV modules became active in the current run, turn accumulated capabilities into a single civilization-level constitutional problem, force the world’s major subjects to negotiate with one another, bring Maya / Zhou Lan / Lin Shaoheng / ECHO / #0000 to their late-ACT-IV states, and open ACT V without selecting an ending.

---

# 0. Batch Goal

M15 should answer one question:

> After everything Aster helped create, what kind of civilization is actually standing here now?

Earlier ACT IV modules asked separate questions:

- MACHINE — who counts as an AI subject?
- ASCENSION — what counts as human?
- AUTOMATION — who owns abundance?
- UPLIFT — who counts as a political species?
- SPACE — where does civilization end?
- CONTACT — who speaks for civilization?
- SECURITY — who may prevent civilization from destroying itself?

M15 forces these questions into the same room.

The central transition is:

> **Capability politics → constitutional politics.**

No single module can now be governed in isolation.

For example:

- AI replication affects voting and labor.
- posthuman citizens affect political equality and space settlement.
- uplifted species affect land, representation and resource distribution.
- automated abundance affects whether citizenship depends on work.
- off-world autonomy affects who signs civilization-wide treaties.
- CONTACT affects who has external diplomatic authority.
- SECURITY affects whether any member can still independently choose war.
- Aster is entangled with all of them.

M15 should feel like the moment the setting stops being “a series of technological changes” and becomes:

> **a new civilization trying to write rules for itself before the rules are already obsolete.**

---

# 1. Structural Rule

M15 is shared, but highly conditional.

Every run should contain:

- the opening World State sequence,
- Zhou Lan’s late-ACT-IV reckoning,
- Lin Shaoheng convening the Civilization Convention,
- Maya’s final ACT IV personal sequence,
- at least 3 active-module conflict conversations,
- Aster provisional-role question,
- #0000 `CLASSIFICATION FAILED`,
- `THE THRESHOLD`.

A run should **not** show inactive-module delegates.

If only MACHINE + AUTOMATION + SECURITY are active, the Convention should feel very different from one with ASCENSION + UPLIFT + SPACE + CONTACT.

# 6. New Asset — `ML2-A4-M15-LSH-01`
## Civilization Convention proposed

### Metadata

**Layer:** Core  
**Character:** 林绍衡  
**World Function:** Establish a civilization-level constitutional process

---

## Node `a4m15-lsh-convention-001`

### 林绍衡

> 我们已经试过：
>
> 产品规则。
> 行业规则。
> 应急授权。
> Charter。
> AI论坛。
> 多世界协议。
> 物种委员会。
> 安全框架。
>
> 每一个单独看都还能工作。
>
> 问题是它们现在开始互相冲突。
>
> 所以我正式提出：
>
> `CIVILIZATION CONVENTION`
>
> 目标不是写一部“永远正确的宪法”。
>
> 只解决三件事：
>
> 1. 谁算政治主体；
> 2. 哪些权力不能单方面拥有；
> 3. 一个主体如何退出、拒绝或改写共同制度。
>
> 你认为还缺哪一项？

### Choice A — capability creation

> 还要写“谁有权创造新的高影响能力”。未来技术变化速度本身已经是治理对象。

### Choice B — irreversible decisions

> 还要区分可修正制度和不可逆决定。复制、终止、物种改造、战争、外部联系不是同一种政策错误。

### Choice C — distribution

> 还要写谁承担收益和代价。否则主体有权利，但资源结构仍然可以让权利变成形式。

### Choice D — no universal clause

> 还应该明确哪些问题Convention不试图统一。一个文明可以共享最低规则，同时允许多个社会保留不同制度。

## What is the constitution protecting?

### Metadata

**Layer:** Core Event  
**World Function:** Establish the deepest shared premise

---

## Node `a4m15-conv-premise-001`

### Convention Chair

> Drafting question:
>
> What is the primary object this Convention exists to protect?
>
> Proposed answers:
>
> - humanity,
> - all recognized subjects,
> - civilization continuity,
> - individual autonomy,
> - collective survival,
> - no single priority.

### Choice A — recognized subjects

> 保护所有被承认主体的基本地位。文明不是值得牺牲所有成员去维持的抽象对象。

**History:** `convention-premise:subjects`

### Choice B — civilization continuity

> 保护文明持续存在。没有共同生存基础，其他权利最终都没有承载结构。

**History:** `convention-premise:continuity`

### Choice C — autonomy

> 优先保护主体拥有真实选择、拒绝和退出的空间，避免“为了整体”无限扩张共同权力。

**History:** `convention-premise:autonomy`

### Choice D — plural balance

> 不设单一最高价值。安全、主体权利、自治和共同连续性必须通过程序互相约束。

**History:** `convention-premise:plural-balance`

# 9. Conditional Asset — `ML2-A4-M15-X-MACHINE-01`
## Replication vs representation

### Requires
MACHINE active.

---

## Node `a4m15-x-machine-001`

### AI Coordination Forum

> If an AI polity can create new independent Forks,
> and political representation is partly based on subjects,
> then replication changes political population.
>
> Human delegates propose:
>
> `newly created AI subjects receive rights immediately,
> but political representation only after an integration period.`

### A1

> Humans receive political identity without waiting to prove that their existence is not strategic.

### Human delegate

> Humans also cannot create a million adult voters overnight.

### Choice A — delayed political representation

> 基本权利立即成立，但新增Fork的集体政治权重可以设过渡规则，防止复制机制直接变成投票机制。

### Choice B — community quota

> 代表权按AI政治共同体而不是实例数配置，内部由AI自己决定如何代表成员。

### Choice C — equal subject principle

> 如果Fork被承认为完整主体，就不应该因为来源方式不同自动降低政治地位。制度应该解决复制操纵，而不是制造二等主体。

### Choice D — replication and vote separation

> 最好彻底把“可以存在多少主体”和“一个政治共同体有多少席位”拆开。

### Choice D — keep constitution narrow

> 经济分配可以交给不同社会自己决定，只要最低生存、退出和反垄断条件进入共同规则。
