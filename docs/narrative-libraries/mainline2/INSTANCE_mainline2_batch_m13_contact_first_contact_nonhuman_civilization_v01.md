## Batch M13 — CONTACT / First Contact & Nonhuman Civilization v0.1

**Status:** Editorial draft for review  
**Scope:** ACT IV / Singularity — CONTACT module  
**Depends on:** M1–M12  
**Purpose:** Develop the rarest ACT IV route from an unresolved deep-space anomaly into verified artificial origin, public disclosure, a choice about whether to reply, the first genuinely nonhuman Conversation, and the first political relationship between human/Aster civilization and an external intelligence.

---

# 0. Module Goal

M13 should answer one question:

> What happens when civilization finally encounters an intelligence that did not come from Earth — and nobody can agree who has the right to answer it?

This module must feel qualitatively different from every earlier route.

Until now, every major conflict came from:
- humans,
- AI systems created by humans,
- uplifted terrestrial life,
- posthuman descendants,
- off-world societies descended from Earth.

CONTACT introduces a participant with **no terrestrial origin**.

The route should escalate in this order:

1. anomaly survives independent verification,
2. natural explanations weaken,
3. structured information is detected,
4. artificial-origin confidence crosses a threshold,
5. disclosure becomes unavoidable,
6. society argues over whether anyone should answer,
7. decoding reveals that the signal contains more than a static message,
8. a locally instantiated diplomatic/interpretive process becomes possible,
9. first `ORIGIN: UNKNOWN` Conversation,
10. external civilization remains ontologically ambiguous,
11. Earth/off-world/AI societies disagree over representation,
12. player chooses a CONTACT DOCTRINE,
13. first diplomatic relationship forms,
14. civilization becomes cosmically plural.

M13 should **not** immediately reveal:
- the full alien civilization,
- fleets arriving at Earth,
- faster-than-light travel,
- a simple “good aliens / evil aliens” answer,
- the alien origin species,
- whether the external intelligence is biological, machine, post-biological, collective, or extinct.

The unknown must stay partially unknown.

# 1. Eligibility

CONTACT should be rare.

Strong prerequisites:

- `contact-seed:deep-space-anomaly`
- `space_industry_limited`
- long-baseline deep-space observation capability
- `autonomous_research`
- high frontier-science investment
- independent verification infrastructure
- sufficient social stability to sustain long-term observation

Additional weights:

- `INTERSTELLAR COMMITMENT`
- MACHINE route active
- ECHO-9 or plural AI systems available for independent interpretation
- `MULTIWORLD FEDERATION`
- `ASTER COORDINATION`
- strong plural-governance history

CONTACT should normally not appear if SPACE never matured.

---

# 2. Core Contact Premise

The external signal is not a plain radio sentence.

It is a deliberately self-describing information structure.

Over time, researchers discover that it contains:

- timing references,
- mathematical invariants,
- error-correction structure,
- sensory-independent symbolic primitives,
- a protocol for building a local interpretive model,
- a bounded “representative process” designed to answer questions using information embedded in the transmission.

This avoids instant FTL conversation.

The first nonhuman Conversation is with a **locally reconstructed diplomatic interface**, not necessarily the distant civilization in real time.

Important uncertainty:

> Is this process an ambassador, a recording, an AI, a cultural archive, a simulation, or something else?

The external source does not provide a clean human category.
