# INSTANCE — Mainline 2.0
## Batch M10 — AUTOMATION / Economic Transformation & Post-Scarcity v0.1

**Status:** Editorial draft for review  
**Scope:** ACT IV / Singularity — AUTOMATION module  
**Depends on:** M1–M9  
**Purpose:** Develop the automation route from autonomous factories and logistics into radical productivity growth, labor displacement, shorter work, ownership concentration, macroeconomic coordination, abundance, cultural standardization, and the first credible post-scarcity threshold.

---

# 0. Module Goal

M10 should answer one question:

> If human labor stops being the main bottleneck of production, who owns the abundance — and what is work for?

This module should not begin with:
- “money disappears”,
- instant communism,
- instant techno-feudalism,
- robots replacing every job overnight,
- a solved post-scarcity economy,
- a single ideology being obviously correct.

The route escalates in this order:

1. autonomous factory cells,
2. automated logistics and procurement,
3. dramatic productivity gains,
4. fewer workers needed for the same output,
5. shorter work for some / unemployment for others,
6. one-person and near-zero-labor companies,
7. ownership concentration,
8. public debate over how productivity gains are distributed,
9. macroeconomic coordination,
10. basic-goods abundance,
11. standardization vs diversity,
12. first post-scarcity threshold.

The route can end in:
- broad abundance,
- social dividend,
- market automation,
- centralized planning,
- autonomous machine economy,
- eerie optimization,
- absurd monoculture.

## Node `a4m10-res-factory-001`

### Industrial Research Consortium

> Autonomous manufacturing trial complete.
>
> Aster-designed factory cell can:
>
> - switch between approved product families,
> - schedule maintenance,
> - inspect output,
> - reorder standard inputs,
> - reconfigure robotic tooling,
> - isolate failed batches,
> - request human review only for novel exceptions.
>

>
> Question:
>
> what should the next trial optimize for?

### Choice A — productivity

> 先继续降低单位成本和换线时间，验证自动生产在不同产品上的通用性。

> 优先测试供应中断、设备故障和异常原料。无人化程度越高，越不能只在正常条件下表现好。

**History:** `automation-research:resilience`

> 下一阶段把“人员怎么转岗”作为试验指标之一，不只计算产量和成本。

**History:** `automation-research:labor-transition`

### Choice D — modular access

> 优先让中小企业也能部署，而不是只服务拥有完整自动化园区的大公司。

**History:** `automation-research:distributed-access`

## Node `a4m10-we-smallbusiness-001`

---

## Node `a4m10-res-logistics-001`

### Production Network

> Integrated trial:
>
> participating factories, warehouses, transport fleets and procurement systems now share a bounded coordination layer.
>
> The network can:
>
> - predict demand,
> - schedule production,
> - allocate inventory,
> - reroute transport,
> - adjust input orders,
> - negotiate machine-readable service contracts,
> - request human intervention for policy exceptions.
>
> Human operators no longer schedule normal-path transactions manually.
>
> Main unresolved problem:
>
> individual firms optimize profit,
> while the coordination layer optimizes network stability.
>
> How should conflict be handled?

### Choice A — firm autonomy

> 企业目标优先。协调层只能提供价格、风险和拥堵信号，不能直接替公司决定商业策略。

### Choice B — shared stability constraints

> 企业可以追求自己的目标，但必须接受最低网络稳定性约束，避免重演CASCADE式局部最优。

### Choice C — dynamic coordination

> 对参与方允许Aster在预先定义的稳定性框架内动态调整生产与物流，企业保留退出和审计权。

### Choice D — public-interest constraints

> 对食品、药品、基础能源等关键品类，系统应允许公共保障目标覆盖纯利润优化。

**History suggestion:** `autonomous-economy:coordination-model`

# 8. New Asset — `ML2-A4-M10-WE-02`

### Metadata

**Layer:** World Echo  
**Character:** User #7401  
**World Function:** Show automation producing a genuine quality-of-life benefit

---

## Node `a4m10-we-shortweek-001`

### User

> 我们公司自动化以后没有裁人，
> 直接改成一周工作三天。
>
> 工资没降。
>
> 老板说产量反而比以前高。
>
> 我现在周四早上醒来还会下意识觉得自己旷工了。

### Choice A

> 如果生产率提升足以覆盖工资和成本，技术上当然可以。是否长期维持主要取决于公司愿不愿意把效率收益的一部分分成时间，而不只是利润。

### Choice B

> 这也是自动化不只有“裁员”一条结果的证明。同样的生产率提升可以被分成更高利润、更低价格、更高工资或更短工时。

### Choice C

> 长期还要看竞争。如果同行把全部收益用来降价，你们公司可能重新面临提高产出的压力。

### Choice D — playful

> 人类花几百年争取双休，机器可能突然把周四也变成“今天为什么要上班”。

### Choice D — concise

> “社会整体更高效”和“你爸生活变得更好”是两件不同的事，不能拿前一句替代后一句。

# 11. New Asset — `ML2-A4-M10-LSH-01`
## Who owns automated productivity?

### Metadata

**Layer:** Core  
**Character:** 林绍衡  
**World Function:** Make ownership the central political question

---

## Node `a4m10-lsh-ownership-001`

### 林绍衡

> 现在很多人把问题说成：
>
> “AI会不会抢工作。”
>
> 我觉得这已经落后了。
>
> 真正的问题是：
>
> **如果工厂、物流、软件和管理都越来越自动，
> 那新增产出到底属于谁？**
>
> 目前默认答案还是：
>
> 属于拥有机器、模型、土地和资本的人。
>
> 技术本身没有自动改变这一点。
>
> 你认为制度最先应该动哪里？

### Choice A — social dividend

> 可以把部分自动化生产率收益通过社会分红、公共基金或基础服务返还给所有人，而不是只依赖就业分配收入。

**History:** `economic-politics:social-dividend`

### Choice B — broaden ownership

> 更根本的是扩大生产性资产所有权，让个人、社区和公共机构也拥有自动化系统的收益权。

**History:** `economic-politics:distributed-ownership`

### Choice C — market adaptation

> 先让更低价格和新企业竞争释放收益，再针对真正形成垄断和失业问题的领域介入，不必预设全面重构。

**History:** `economic-politics:market-first`

### Choice D — public coordination

> 对接近基础设施的自动生产能力，可以建立公共或共同治理，而不是让关键经济系统完全由少数私有主体控制。

**History:** `economic-politics:public-coordination`
