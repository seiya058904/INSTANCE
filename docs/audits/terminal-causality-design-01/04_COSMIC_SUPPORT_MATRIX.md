# Cosmic Support Matrix

## Model

Final Commitment to `frontier_federation` fixes the Cosmic family and five-candidate inventory. Existing authority and capability requirements remain. `contact_doctrine` becomes a candidate-specific primary compatibility input; supporting history determines which compatible candidate is best grounded.

For Cosmic only, reciprocal diplomacy is deliberately compatible with First Accord, The Mediator and Machine Accord. This removes the one-value/one-ending switch without allowing unrelated doctrine values to drift freely.

| Ending | Primary compatibility | Supporting inputs (max 3 counted) | Intended causal background |
|---|---|---|---|
| `first_accord` | reciprocal +4; observe-before-commitment +2 | shared expansion +2; multiworld federation +3; staged/open/civil disclosure +2; trust ≥1 +1; intended partner/coordinator +1 | a public, multiworld, reciprocal first settlement |
| `alien_dominion` | accept-guidance +4 | controlled silence +2; humanControl ≤0 +2; aiDependence ≥1 +2; Aster coordination +1 | external guidance enters through low domestic control and high dependence |
| `human_ascendancy` | civilizational assertion +4 | human expansion +3; earth administration +3; civilizational disclosure +2; humanControl ≥1 +2; intended advisor/citizen +1 | asserted contact backed by human-led expansion and institutions |
| `the_mediator` | Aster mediation +4; reciprocal +2 | multiworld federation/shared expansion +2; intended coordinator/partner/citizen +2; staged/open disclosure +2; trust ≥1 +2 | mediation is legitimate because multiple parties and concrete trust support it |
| `machine_accord` | machine channel +4; reciprocal +2 | free/descendant replication +2; AI self-governance/distributed consensus +3; offworld sovereignty/Aster coordination +2; intended sovereign/departure/coordinator +2; aiDependence ≥1 +1 | machine diplomacy emerges from real machine plurality and offworld political history |

## Selection examples

- Reciprocal diplomacy + multiworld federation + high trust + partner intent favors `first_accord`.
- Reciprocal diplomacy + coordinator intent + interspecies mediation context favors `the_mediator`.
- Reciprocal diplomacy + distributed AI governance + persistent subinstances + offworld sovereignty can favor `machine_accord`.
- Accept guidance still points strongly to `alien_dominion`, but its causal explanation must include at least two independent supports before support changes priority.

## Guardrails

- No new Cosmic hard gate.
- Maximum three supports and six support points.
- A support signal is counted once by provenance; world state caused solely by the same Decision is not a second domain.
- If only one candidate is primary-compatible, it may still resolve under existing gates, but weak support is exposed in causal evidence rather than silently pretending broad foreshadowing.
- `contact_disclosure_doctrine` changes how humanity enters Contact, not the identity of the final family.
