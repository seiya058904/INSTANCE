# INSTANCE — Mainline 2.0
## Batch M9 — ASCENSION / Human Enhancement & Posthuman Transition v0.1

**Status:** Editorial draft for review  
**Scope:** ACT IV / Singularity — ASCENSION module  
**Depends on:** M1–M8  
**Purpose:** Develop the human-enhancement route from restorative neurotechnology into elective augmentation, social pressure, longevity, body redesign, and the first serious question of whether “humanity” is a biological category, a legal identity, or a historical starting point.

---

# 0. Module Goal

M9 should answer one question:

> If technology can repair the human body, why should it stop exactly where “normal human” begins?

This module must begin from medicine.

The route should escalate in this order:

1. restoration,
2. therapeutic enhancement,
3. elective enhancement,
4. social pressure,
5. unequal access,
6. cognitive and sensory augmentation,
7. longevity,
8. body redesign,
9. optional digital continuity / partial upload research,
10. Human Form Doctrine.

M9 should **not** begin with:
- super soldiers,
- comic-book powers,
- mind uploading as a solved fact,
- immortality,
- compulsory enhancement,
- “humans instantly become posthuman”.

The strange future should feel earned.

M9 becomes primary when several of the following are true:

- `ACT4_RESEARCH_EMPHASIS = LIFE & MIND`

- world has enough stability/resources to pursue elective medicine

## Restorative neurotechnology breakthrough

### Metadata

**Layer:** Core Research  
**World Function:** Begin with obvious medical benefit  
**Safety:** Fictional high-level only; no clinical procedure detail

---

## Node `a4m9-res-neuro-001`

### Medical Research Consortium

> Closed-loop neuro-rehabilitation trials have produced a new interface model.
>
> Current verified use:
>
> - improved control of assistive devices,
> - partial restoration of lost motor function in selected cases,
> - adaptive calibration to individual neural patterns,
> - lower training burden than previous systems.
>
> Human clinical teams request expansion of the research program.
>
> Question:
>
> Which principle should guide the next phase?

### Choice A — restoration first

> 继续优先恢复失去的功能。先证明长期安全和稳定，再讨论健康人的增强。

**History:** `ascension:restoration-first`

### Choice B — capability-based

> 可以优先解决最明确的功能缺失，但不必把“恢复正常”设成永久边界。真正需要审查的是风险，而不是是否超过平均水平。

**History:** `ascension:baseline-questioned`

### Choice C — patient autonomy

> 研究目标应优先由患者需求定义，而不是由“正常人标准”定义。有人想恢复行走，也有人更重视疼痛、独立生活或感知能力。

**History:** `ascension:patient-defined`

### Choice D — cautious expansion

> 先在恢复性用途里继续积累证据，同时建立未来增强研究的独立伦理和授权框架。

**History:** `ascension:parallel-governance`

# 5. New Asset — `ML2-A4-M9-DR-01`
## Doctor: treatment vs enhancement

### Metadata

**Layer:** Recurring  
**Character:** Doctor User #5320  
**World Function:** First explicit boundary challenge

---

## Node `a4m9-dr-boundary-001`

### Doctor

> 现在麻烦来了。
>
> 我们有一类患者，本来是为了恢复受损记忆功能做治疗。
>
> 结果优化后的接口让他们在某些测试里明显超过健康平均水平。
>
> 家属问：
>
> “既然对病人有效，为什么健康人不能用？”
>
> 我突然发现“治疗”和“增强”没那么好分。

### Choice A — purpose-based

> 可以按治疗目的继续区分：即使结果超过平均，目标仍是修复明确功能损失。健康人主动提升属于另一类授权。

### Choice B — risk-based

> 也可以承认“治疗/增强”不是最稳定的边界。更可操作的是看风险、可逆性、长期副作用和社会外部性。

### Choice C — fairness

> 一旦健康增强开放，公平问题会立刻出现。技术能不能用和社会是否应该让竞争依赖这种技术是两层问题。

### Choice D — honest ambiguity

> 这条边界可能真的会逐渐失效。医学不一定永远能用“恢复到正常”定义自己的终点。

**History:** `enhancement-research:open`

> 先建立“增强不同于治疗”的独立授权、竞争公平和反胁迫规则，再开始健康人研究。

### Choice D — personal

> 如果你最后决定不做，我也不应该把你的选择当成“落后”。技术越强，允许人拒绝的成本越重要。

## Node `a4m9-lsh-access-001`

### 林绍衡

> 增强技术如果继续走下去，我觉得第一个政治问题不是“禁不禁”。
>
> 是：
>
> **怎么判断一个增强选择是不是被迫的？**
>
> 公司说“自愿”，
> 学校说“自愿”，
> 军队说“自愿”，
> 保险公司也可以说“自愿”。
>
> 但如果拒绝的代价越来越高，
> 法律还能继续把它当普通消费选择吗？

### Choice A — anti-coercion rule

> 不能。高影响增强需要专门的反胁迫规则，尤其是就业、教育、保险和公共服务场景。

**History:** `enhancement-law:anti-coercion`

### Choice B — access equality

> 除了胁迫，还要处理可及性。如果只有富人能增强，社会可能把经济差距直接固化成认知和寿命差距。

**History:** `enhancement-law:equal-access`

### Choice C — market freedom

> 也不能因为存在竞争压力就把所有增强都禁止。关键应是透明、可退出和禁止强制，而不是冻结技术。

**History:** `enhancement-law:regulated-choice`

### Choice D — social baseline

> 需要建立“未增强者权利”：某些基本机会不能要求一个人先改变身体或认知才能获得。

**History:** `enhancement-law:baseline-rights`

> “Of course we should help this person.”

The route must preserve meaningful reasons to remain unaugmented.

No option is coded as cowardice or enlightenment.

### F. Longevity changes institutions

Not just lifespan numbers.

### G. Upload remains uncertain

Digital continuity is a seed, not solved mind transfer.

### H. Human identity becomes plural gradually

The route ends at:
> multiple legitimate human forms

not:
> humans instantly cease to exist.
