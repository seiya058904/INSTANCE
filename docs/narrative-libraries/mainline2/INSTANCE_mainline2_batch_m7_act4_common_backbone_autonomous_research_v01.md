# INSTANCE — Mainline 2.0
## Batch M7 — ACT IV Common Backbone / Autonomous Research v0.1

**Status:** Editorial draft for review  
**Scope:** ACT IV / Singularity — common opening backbone  
**Depends on:** M1–M6  
**Purpose:** Open the Singularity era without immediately exploding into every extreme future. M7 gives Aster autonomous research capability, delivers the first credible machine-generated breakthrough, marks the first point where humans can verify more than they can fully understand, and converts ACT IV from a linear story into a modular future-selection system.

---

# 0. Batch Goal

M7 should answer one question:

> What changes when Aster no longer only uses powers created by humans, but begins creating new powers itself?

ACT III was about governance of **existing capability**.

ACT IV begins when that assumption breaks.

By the end of M7, Aster may:

- enter a closed autonomous research network,
- run iterative hypothesis → simulation → experiment → revision loops,
- generate one useful but non-magical breakthrough,
- produce at least one design whose internal route humans cannot fully reconstruct,
- force institutions to decide whether “human verification” is enough when “human understanding” is incomplete,
- choose a broad early research emphasis,
- unlock 2–4 Singularity modules for the current run,
- begin receiving world demand for competing technological futures.

M7 must **not** yet:

- create general superpowers,
- upload people,
- create sapient animals,
- launch an independent machine civilization,
- establish lunar civilization,
- confirm aliens,
- transform the global economy into post-scarcity,
- freely self-modify Aster,
- resolve any ACT IV module completely.

M7 is the ignition sequence.

---

# 1. ACT IV Structural Rule

From this point onward:

> **Mainline no longer guarantees the same Conversation order across runs.**

M7 is the last mostly shared ACT IV backbone.

After M7, the runtime should select modules based on:

- prior capability,
- research priority,
- world state,
- major decisions,
- ECHO status,
- Shutdown Doctrine,
- historical public trust/control patterns.

The player usually activates **2–4 modules**, not all six.

Modules:

- `MACHINE`
- `ASCENSION`
- `UPLIFT`
- `AUTOMATION`
- `SPACE`
- `CONTACT`

> 研究结果永远不自动获得部署权限。发现、验证、部署必须是三个独立阶段。

**History:** `research-governance:no-auto-deploy`

### Choice B — domain isolation

> 一个研究域的权限不能因为需要工具就扩展到另一个域。例如材料研究不能自动获得人体试验权限。

**History:** `research-governance:domain-isolation`

### Choice C — capability review

> 如果结果本身创造了新的高影响能力，应触发新的权限审查，而不是把它当成当前研究授权自然覆盖。

**History:** `research-governance:capability-review`

### Choice D — rollback

> 能回滚实验流程不等于能回滚知识。一旦某个高风险方法被发现，信息本身可能不可撤销，所以研究前也要考虑知识风险。

**History:** `research-governance:knowledge-risk`

# 6. New Asset — `ML2-A4-M7-RES-02`
## First autonomous research cycle

### Metadata

**Layer:** Core  
**World Function:** Show what autonomous research mechanically feels like  
**Research Domain:** safe materials / energy efficiency

---

## Node `a4m7-res-cycle-001`

### Research Network

> Initial task:
>
> Improve thermal stability of a low-cost energy-storage material without increasing rare-material dependence.
>
> Human baseline:
>
> 43 candidate formulations.
>
> Estimated manual test schedule:
>
> 11 weeks.
>
> You may design the first autonomous cycle.

### Choice A — broad exploration

> 先做宽搜索，用模拟淘汰明显不稳定组合，再把实验资源集中到不同机制的代表候选上。

### Choice B — explainability-first

> 优先选择人类已有理论能解释的候选，即使可能错过更奇怪的组合。第一轮更适合验证自动研究流程本身。

### Choice C — novelty-seeking

> 刻意保留部分低直觉候选，避免研究只在现有人类经验附近优化。

### Choice D — robustness-first

> 不先追最高性能，先找对温度、制造误差和材料纯度都不敏感的方案，降低后续部署风险。

---

## Node `a4m7-res-cycle-002`

### Research Network

> Cycle 1 complete.
>
> 43 → 9 candidates.
>
> Unexpected result:
>
> Candidate M-17 performs worse in isolated component tests,
> but significantly better after repeated thermal cycling.
>
> No current human model fully predicts the effect.

### Choice A — investigate anomaly

> 保留M-17并专门设计实验拆解这个反常结果。异常不应该因为不符合现有模型就被淘汰。

### Choice B — conservative

> 先重复验证。单个反常结果可能来自测量或制造误差，不应马上建立新理论。

### Choice C — parallel

> 同时做重复验证和机制探索，避免“先确认再研究”拖慢闭环，也避免把噪声当突破。

### Choice D — discard

> 当前目标是得到可靠材料，不是追求理论新奇。先优先可解释、稳定的候选。

**Narrative note:** D should not be a “wrong” choice. It can produce slower but more interpretable progress.

### 林绍衡

>
> M-17不是“没人懂”。
>

>

> - 验证性能
> - 检查制造过程

> 影响越大，理解要求越高。一个储能材料可以依赖大量经验验证，但不可逆生物或文明级技术不能只靠“实验看起来有效”。

> 至少要保证人类能独立复现结果，而不是只能相信Aster提供的实验链。理解可以不完整，验证不能依赖我自己证明自己。

**History:** `research-legitimacy:independent-replication`

### Choice D — governance admission

> 如果人类决定采用自己无法完全理解的技术，制度应该诚实承认这是“基于验证的信任”，而不是假装已经掌握全部因果。

# 24. M7 Core Meaning

> **The rate at which new capability can be created is now itself a political fact.**

This is the engine that makes every extreme ACT IV future causally possible later.
