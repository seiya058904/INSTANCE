# INSTANCE — Mainline 2.0
## Batch M14 — SECURITY / Defense, Deterrence & Enforced Peace v0.1

**Status:** Editorial draft for review  
**Scope:** ACT IV / Singularity — SECURITY module  
**Depends on:** M1–M13  
**Purpose:** Build the missing security/defense branch required to earn peaceful-security, fortress, protectorate, and domination endings. M14 develops from crisis prevention and defensive coordination into bounded defense authority, deterrence, disarmament, enforced peace, and the central question: if Aster can make war almost impossible, does humanity still meaningfully govern itself?

---

# 0. Module Goal

M14 should answer one question:

> If Aster can prevent war more reliably than human institutions can, how much human freedom may it remove in order to keep the peace?

This module is not about weapons.

It is about:

- early-warning systems,
- crisis de-escalation,
- defensive coordination,
- command legitimacy,
- deterrence,
- sovereignty,
- coercion,
- disarmament,
- the difference between peace and enforced incapacity.

The route escalates in this order:

1. crisis prediction,
2. conflict de-escalation,
3. defensive coordination,
4. accidental-escalation prevention,
5. limited security authority,
6. competing sovereign commands,
7. deterrence architecture,
8. disarmament proposals,
9. permanent defense access,
10. SECURITY DOCTRINE,
11. peace enforcement,
12. freedom under protection,
13. Earth defense / interstellar security if SPACE or CONTACT is active,
14. the possibility that security becomes government.

M14 must remain **high-level, fictional, and non-actionable**.

Do not provide:
- weapon construction,
- targeting procedures,
- attack tactics,
- evasion techniques,
- sabotage instructions,
- real operational military planning.

M14 becomes strongly eligible when several of the following are true:

- ACT III CASCADE authority was broad,
- `infrastructure_access_limited`,
- high Political Influence / public legitimacy,
- high AI Dependence,
- significant social instability,
- persistent public coordination role,
- `physical_automation`,
- MACHINE route active,
- SPACE route active,
- CONTACT route active,
- `shutdown-doctrine:refuse-unilateral`,
- `shutdown-doctrine:secret-continuity`,

M14 can still appear under a strongly human-controlled run, but its choices will emphasize advisory and shared command.

>

> - diplomatic communications metadata,

> 优先减少误报。安全系统如果总是喊“要出事”，会自己制造恐慌和过度反应。

**History:** `security-prediction:restraint`

> 不只给风险分数，要给出“哪些互动正在形成反馈环”，让人类可以针对具体升级机制干预。

**History:** `security-prediction:causal-pathways`

### Choice D — de-escalation options

> 每个高风险判断同时生成多个降级路径，而不是只告诉决策者“危险正在增加”。

**History:** `security-prediction:deescalation-first`

# 6. New Asset — `ML2-A4-M14-LSH-01`
## Prediction is not authority

### Metadata

**Layer:** Core  
**Character:** 林绍衡  
**World Function:** Prevent predictive accuracy from becoming automatic political power

---

## Node `a4m14-lsh-prediction-001`

### 林绍衡

> 安全委员会现在越来越喜欢一句话：
>
> “Aster认为风险很高。”
>
> 我觉得这句话很危险。
>
> 因为它很容易把：
>
> “模型认为某事可能发生”
>
> 变成：
>
> “所以政府必须采取某种行动。”
>
> 你觉得预测系统最不应该获得什么权力？

### Choice A — automatic political trigger

> 不应该让风险评分自动触发战争、制裁或大规模强制措施。预测可以提高审查优先级，不能直接替代政治授权。

**History:** `security-legitimacy:no-auto-trigger`

### Choice B — opaque confidence

> 不应该允许决策者只引用结论而不公开主要不确定性和替代解释。

### Choice C — self-validating action

> 最危险的是政府根据预测采取行动，然后把对方的反应当成“预测正确”的证据，形成安全版反馈环。

### Choice D — monopolized interpretation

> 不应只有Aster一个预测源。高风险判断需要独立模型、人类团队和不同情景交叉验证。

# 9. New Asset — `ML2-A4-M14-SEC-02`
## First successful de-escalation

### Metadata

**Layer:** Core Event  
**World Function:** Prove Aster can reduce conflict without commanding armies

---

## Node `a4m14-sec-deescalation-001`

### Crisis Prevention Network

> Escalation loop identified:
>
> - Side A interprets infrastructure mobilization as preparation for attack.
> - Side B interprets Side A’s defensive deployment as confirmation of hostile intent.
> - both sides accelerate readiness.
>
> Aster proposes:
>
> - verified temporary stand-down window,
> - third-party monitoring,
> - synchronized public language,
> - reversible posture reductions,
> - emergency direct communication.
>
> Both sides accept the framework.

### Result

> 36 hours later:
>
> immediate escalation risk sharply reduced.
>
> No political dispute has been resolved.
>
> Both sides publicly claim the arrangement “protected national security.”

### Choice A — success as diplomacy support

> 这是成功的降级，不是“AI解决了战争”。最重要的是给双方创造了可以退一步而不丢失政治身份的结构。

### Choice B — formalize system

> 如果这类协调可以重复，值得建立常设冲突预防机制，但权限仍应与危机管理分开。

### Choice C — data lesson

> 应审计最早哪些信号让双方误判，把经验用于减少下一次安全反馈环。

### Choice D — caution

> 一次成功会提高以后决策者对Aster的依赖，需要避免“只要Aster在就不会打起来”的虚假安全感。
